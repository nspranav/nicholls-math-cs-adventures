Explorer’s notebook

Map-reading refresher

Start at (0, 0). Move horizontally by x, then vertically by y. Positive x goes right; positive y goes up. Reflecting across the y-axis changes x to −x. A translation adds the stated horizontal and vertical shifts.

You’re using coordinate geometry. Ordered pairs let us describe locations precisely in maps, graphics, and games.
